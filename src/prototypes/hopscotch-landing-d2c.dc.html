FAQ

Good to know.

How much does it cost?

One scoped project build cost that covers design, build, and migration. We give you the number up front — no retainers, no metered access.

How long does a build take?

Most engagements run six to ten weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on the size of your current system and the migration involved.

What happens to our data on Shopify or our current platform?

We migrate your storefront, customers, and order history onto your new stack — nothing lost, no downtime for your store.

Do we actually own it afterward?

Yes. When the build is done, the code, accounts, and data all transfer to you and your team. You run it going forward.
